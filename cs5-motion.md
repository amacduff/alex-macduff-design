<section id="two" class="spotlights">
	<section>
		<div class="image {{ page.style }}">
			<img src="img/motion-1.png" alt="" data-position="top center" />
		</div>
		<div class="content">
			<div class="inner">
				<header class="major">
					<h3>The Challenge</h3>
				</header>
					<p><strong>The Problem:</strong> The Motion Industries website offered a vast catalog of industrial parts, but even with robust search and filtering, customers struggled to find the exact part they needed. This led to a frustrating and overwhelming user experience.</p>
					<ul>
						<li><strong>Result:</strong> A difficult and time-consuming search process, hindering customer efficiency.</li>
						<li><strong>Core Issue:</strong> There was no guided experience to help users navigate a multi-tiered selection process for specific parts.</li>
					</ul>
			</div>
		</div>
	</section>
	<section>
		<div class="image {{ page.style }}">
			<img src="img/motion-2.png" alt="" data-position="top center" />
		</div>
		<div class="content">
			<div class="inner">
				<header class="major">
					<h3>My Role</h3>
				</header>
					<p>I served as the <strong>UX and visual designer</strong>, responsible for the end-to-end design of a new product configurator feature.</p>
					<ul>
						<li><strong>My Contribution:</strong> I translated business requirements and design references into a user-friendly, multi-step experience with a focus on clear feedback and intuitive interaction design.</li>
					</ul>
			</div>
		</div>
	</section>
	<section>
		<div class="image {{ page.style }}">
			<img src="img/motion-3.png" alt="" data-position="25% 25%" />
		</div>
		<div class="content">
			<div class="inner">
				<header class="major">
					<h3>The Process</h3>
				</header>
					<p>My approach was to create a simple, guided journey through a complex product hierarchy.</p>
					<ul>
						<li><strong>Requirements & Research:</strong> I began with client requirements and research into alternative configurator experiences to identify best practices.</li>
						<li><strong>Interaction Design:</strong> I prioritized clear <strong>visual cues</strong> and <strong>feedback mechanisms</strong> to guide the user. This included designing a simple progress indicator to live at the top of the screen, providing constant reassurance that the user was moving closer to their goal.</li>
						<li><strong>Iterative Design:</strong> I worked within an existing design system and a tight timeline, creating designs for a responsive experience that would work seamlessly on various devices.</li>
					</ul>
			</div>
		</div>
	</section>
	<section>
		<div class="image {{ page.style }}">
			<img src="img/motion-4.png" alt="" data-position="25% 25%" />
		</div>
		<div class="content">
			<div class="inner">
				<header class="major">
					<h3>The Solution</h3>
				</header>
					<p>I designed a <strong>multi-step configurator feature</strong> that simplified a complex product search into a guided process. The solution clearly indicated required selections and showed users their progress, transforming an overwhelming task into a manageable one.</p>
			</div>
		</div>
	</section>
	<section>
		<div class="image {{ page.style }}">

		<div class="content">
			<div class="inner">
				<header class="major">
					<h3>The Impact</h3>
				</header>
					<p>The product configurator was an immediate success.</p>
					<ul>
						<li><strong>The Result:</strong> The Motion Industries team was so pleased with its performance and user feedback that they began to explore applying a similar <strong>guided-experience strategy</strong> to other areas of the site, validating the success of the design approach.</li>
					</ul>
			</div>
		</div>
